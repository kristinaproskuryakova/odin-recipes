Burger with pork patty, blue cheese and pear

About the dish

A juicy pork burger with oozy, melting blue cheese, crisp slices of sweet pear and a fresh, peppery salad, all tucked into a soft toasted bun. The combination of salty, creamy cheese and sweet, crunchy pear may sound unexpected, but one bite and you’ll wonder why you never tried it before. And best of all, it’s on the table in under 20 minutes, perfect for a weeknight treat or a lazy weekend lunch!

Ingredients (for 2 portions)

  • 1 ripe sweet pear
  • 100g mixed spinach, rocket & watercress
  • 300g higher-welfare minced pork
  • 2 soft burger buns
  • 30g soft blue cheese
  • Plus: extra virgin olive oil, red wine vinegar, salt and black pepper

Follow the steps

  1. Slice the pear lengthways very thinly. Dress it with the salad leaves, a splash of olive oil and red wine vinegar, and a pinch of black pepper.
  2. Season the minced pork, mix it with your hands and shape into patties about 1 cm thick.
  3. Rub the patty with a teaspoon of olive oil and cook it in a large non-stick frying pan over high heat for 2 minutes. Meanwhile, toast the halved bun in the same pan until golden.
  4. Flip the burger. After 2 more minutes, crumble the blue cheese into the pan beside it and let it melt.
  5. Place the burger on top of the melted cheese so it gets coated, then transfer it to the bottom half of the bun.
  6. Pile on as much pear salad as you can, add the bun lid, press down gently and serve any leftover salad on the side.
