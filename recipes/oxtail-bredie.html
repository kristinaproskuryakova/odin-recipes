Oxtail Tomato Bredie

About the dish

A rich, slow-simmered South African classic: meltingly tender oxtail in a sweet and savoury tomato gravy with soft chunks of sweet potato. It’s the kind of comforting dish that fills the whole house with an irresistible aroma, perfect for a cosy Sunday lunch or a chilly evening with someone special. And best of all, it practically cooks itself in one pot while you get on with your day!

Ingredients (for 4 portions)

  • 1 kg oxtail
  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes, blanched and peeled
  • 800 g tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 2 large onions, chopped
  • 2 large sweet potatoes, sliced
  • Oil, for frying
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Brown sugar, to taste
  • Fresh basil, to serve (optional)
  • 1–2 tbsp tomato paste, to thicken (if needed)

Follow the steps

  1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan and brown the oxtail on all sides.
  2. Take the oxtail out of the pan, add the onions and fry until soft.
  3. Return the oxtail to the pan along with the fresh and tinned tomatoes, salt and pepper. Cover and cook over low heat for about 3 hours (or in the oven at 120°C). If the sauce is too tart, add a little brown sugar.
  4. Add the sweet potatoes and cook for another 30 minutes. If the gravy is too thin, stir in some tomato paste.
  5. Serve with rice and garnish with basil, if you like.
