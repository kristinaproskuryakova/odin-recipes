Lamb Chops with baby potato and peppercorn sauce

About the dish

Incredibly succulent lamb chops with golden roasted baby potatoes, wilted spinach and a rich peppercorn sauce. This is a dish that shines just as brightly at a festive table for a crowd as it does at a quiet dinner for two with someone you love. And best of all, you'll need just one pan and no oil to make it!

Ingredients (for 2 portions)

  • 6 pcs lamb chops
  • 500g baby potato
  • 300g spinach
  • 4-5 cloves of garlic
  • Few sprigs of thyme
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

For the peppercorn sauce

  • 120ml fresh cream 33%
  • 100ml chicken stock
  • 1,5 tbsp green/black pepper
  • 1 shallots
  • 20g butter
  • Salt, to taste

Follow the steps

  1. Wash the baby potatoes and boil them for 7–8 minutes, then cut them in half.
  2. Score the fat on the lamb chops (on the bone side) with shallow cuts. Rub with salt and pepper, working it well into the cuts in the fat.
  3. Place the lamb fat-side down in a cold pan and set it over low heat.
  4. Once the fat starts to render, turn up the hit, add the garlic cloves, unpeeled and crushed with the flat side of a knife. Cook until the fat is golden and crisp.
  5. Add the thyme, then sear the lamb for 2–2½ minutes on each side.
  6. Transfer to a plate, cover loosely with foil and let it rest for 5–10 minutes.
  7. In the remaining fat, fry the boiled baby potatoes for 5–7 minutes until golden.
  8. Add the spinach to the same pan and cook for 2–3 minutes until wilted.
  9. For the peppercorn sauce melt the butter in a saucepan and sauté the finely chopped shallot and crushed peppercorns for 1–2 minutes.
  10. Pour in the stock and reduce by half (3–4 minutes).
  11. Stir in the cream, season with salt and simmer for 5–6 minutes until thickened.
